Alphosynth

Type letters and numbers to create sounds and rhythms.

How it works

  • a‑g (lowercase) → C4‑B4 notes
  • A‑G (uppercase) → C5‑B5 notes (one octave up)
  • 0 → Kick drum
  • 1 → Snare drum
  • 2 → Closed hi‑hat
  • 3 → Open hi‑hat
  • 4 → Low tom
  • 5 → High tom
  • 6 → Clap
  • 7 → Cowbell
  • 8 → Rimshot
  • 9 → Tambourine
  • Space → Rest (no sound)
  • Enter → Start/stop recording mode (visual cue)

Press Play to loop your recorded sequence. Adjust tempo to change speed.
